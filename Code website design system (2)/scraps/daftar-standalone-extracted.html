§ I — The Thesis

One practitioner. Senior attention. Direct.

Most companies under SAR 500 million in revenue cannot justify a Big Four engagement fee — and even if they pay one, the work itself rarely reaches the partner's desk. It flows down the pyramid.

Daftar is built for that gap. One auditor, dual Big Four trained, doing the technical work directly. No team of five. No managed handoff. The numbers the partner would have signed off on — produced by the person who would have prepared them anyway.

Fixed scope. Fixed fee. The deliverable lands with the working file underneath it — formulas, sources, every number tied. Yours to keep.
