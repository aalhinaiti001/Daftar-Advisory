§ 01 · The idea

Rigorous finance, without the overhead.

Daftar means the ledger or register. The firm does the work properly, writes it down in a form that holds up, and hands it over so the client can run it again. Calibre is that discipline made into a product, and always carries the by Daftar kicker.

  1. 01Write it down. If a decision can't be written in a form that holds up, it isn't finished.
  2. 02Evidence over opinion. Show the working rather than defend a verdict on authority.
  3. 03One title, everywhere. Ahmad is Founder on every surface.
  4. 04Leave a tool behind. Every engagement ends with something the client can run themselves.
§ 02 · The mark

The file the client keeps.

An ink page with its corner turned down in rust. Daftar means the ledger, and every engagement ends with a file the client keeps. The mark replaces the D monogram tile on every Daftar surface.

100-unit square. A page with a cut corner is the universal file icon; the rust corner is what makes it Daftar's, so every rule protects it. Clear space is the height of the fold on every side. Minimum 16px.

§ 03 · Colour

Paper, ink, and five per cent rust.

§ 04 · Type

Two faces. One to read, one to measure.

Fraunces sets everything that reads. JetBrains Mono sets everything that labels or measures. A third Latin face in a Daftar design means the design is wrong.

AaFraunces

Display, headings, body, buttons, navigation and the wordmark. Weights 300–700. The italic is the accent voice.

AaJetBrains Mono

Eyebrows, § chapter marks, service codes, register figures, footers. Weight 400, uppercase, tracked.

§ 05 · Voice

Say the thing others hedge.

Plainly, with the working shown. If a sentence wouldn't sound natural from a senior auditor explaining it over coffee, rewrite it.

We are

Direct · specific ("SAR 3B portfolio") · technical when it matters · honest about trade-offs · bilingual when useful. "We" is the firm, never a headcount; no invented team.

We avoid

Leverage · synergy · cutting-edge · world-class · unlock · empower · disrupt · best-in-class · journey. Outcome promises. Superlatives the record hasn't earned. The same words in Arabic.

§ 06 · Layout, icons, motion

Ruled, not shadowed.

Layout

1px rules divide sections. Base-8 spacing: 8 · 16 · 24 · 48 · 80. Content to 1120px. Radii 5–6px. Eyebrows lead with § in mono. Left-align Latin, right-align Arabic.

Icons

24px grid, 1.5px stroke at every size, round caps, no fills. Ink, with the accent on one active mark. Always paired with a mono label. Optional on the web.

Motion

One ease, Settle cubic-bezier(.2,.8,.2,1). 120 tap · 200 hover · 320 reveal · 560 page. Fade and 8px rise. No bounce, no spinners. Respect reduced motion.

§ 07 · Surfaces

The same page, at every size.

Web

One language switch: "العربية" on English pages, "EN" in mono on Arabic pages, never a flag. hreflang on both versions, each with its own og:url. OG card 1200×630, paper, ink and rust.

Social

1080 square, 1350 portrait, 1920 story, 72px margin. № series kicker top-left, one accent word, lockup bottom-left. Three posts a week. Never both brands' marks on one post.

Print

Fraunces body at 10.5–11pt, mono labels, paper ground, rust as rules and the single accent word. Letters, statements and brochures carry the horizontal lockup.

§ 08 · Calibre

The product keeps its own ground.

Calibre keeps its approved white-and-forest system with Lora and Plus Jakarta Sans; the two-face rule is Daftar's. The two grounds never meet on one surface.

CCalibreby Daftar

The offer is the Calibre Verdict: a written finance-hiring decision memo for a defined shortlist. Read, Score, Compare, Calibrate. Never a ranking, a fit score or a prediction.

Use statement · verbatim

Structured hiring advisory for finance roles. Not a psychometric assessment. Does not predict performance. The decision, and its consequences, rest with the employer.
