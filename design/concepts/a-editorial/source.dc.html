One standard, written down.

The name daftar, n. A ledger; a register; the book where things are written down and kept.

We write the work down so it holds up, and hand it over so your team can run it again.

Most advisory work leaves a report and little else. Ours ends with something your team can use: a model they can rerun, a method they can repeat, or a memo that answers the question for good.
