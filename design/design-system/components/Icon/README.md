Icons sit on a 24px grid with a 1.5px stroke at every size, round caps and joins, and no fills. They are drawn in `ink`; one active mark may take `rust`.

- Always pair an icon with a mono label (the `eyebrow` style). Icons are optional on the web.
- The six drawings here (file, ledger, tie-out, calendar, tool, evidence) are an extension: the brand fixes the rules, not the drawings. Draw new icons to the same rules.
- Inline the SVG so it can take `ink` or `rust`; as `<img>` it cannot inherit colour.
