The Daftar mark and horizontal lockup as fixed-colour SVG artwork. Use these files as given; never redraw or recolour them. Geometry follows the guide and the Mark 1D recommendation of 11 October 2026.

- `daftar-mark.svg`: Standard cut (fold 64/36), ink page, rust corner. Light grounds, 24px and up.
- `daftar-mark-on-ink.svg`: Standard cut, paper page, corner stays `#A8341F`. Ink grounds, 24px and up. No tile is baked in.
- `daftar-mark-small.svg`: Small cut (fold 54/46), ink page. Below 24px; minimum 16px.
- `daftar-mark-small-on-ink.svg`: Small cut, paper page, corner `#A8341F`.
- `daftar-favicon.svg`: Small cut on a paper tile with a hairline, per the guide's compact icon.
- `daftar-lockup-horizontal.svg`: mark, hairline and wordmark with outlined text, for paper grounds.
- `daftar-lockup-horizontal-on-ink.svg`: knockout lockup for ink grounds, "Advisory." in `#E07458`.
- `daftar-og-card.svg` and `.png`: 1200 × 630 social card, horizontal lockup on paper.

Choose the cut by the rendered mark size. Keep clear space equal to the fold height: 0.32 × size for Standard, 0.42 × size for Small. The fold's diagonal edge against an ink ground is below 3:1; it is an accepted brand-mark exception. The D monogram is retired.
