About

A finance practice built to stay direct, focused, and useful.

This page explains the fit in simple terms instead of trying to say everything at once.

Who this is for

Growing businesses that need senior finance support.

Daftar Advisory is a good fit when a founder, CFO, or finance lead needs real help with an important file, number, or accounting issue.

  • Founder-led companies preparing for growth
  • Finance teams handling new reporting pressure
  • Businesses going into audit or diligence
  • Leaders who want a direct answer, not a large team
How the practice works

Small by design.

The structure is meant to reduce noise. Clients speak directly with the person doing the work, and the work is shaped around a defined problem.

  • Senior-led work from start to finish
  • Clear engagement boundaries
  • Less handoff, less confusion
  • Practical files your team can keep using
Why clients come back

Three reasons the model works.

The message here is simple: direct work, clear structure, and practical output.

Reason 01

Direct contact

The person you speak with is the person doing the work.

Reason 02

Clear scope

The engagement is defined before it starts, so there is less drift and less waste.

Reason 03

Useful output

The final file should help the client move forward, not create more dependency.

Short version

Daftar Advisory is not trying to be everything. It is built for focused finance work where clarity, judgment, and follow-through matter.
