§ 01 — The house

A register, and the method it made.

Daftar — daf·tar, the ledger or register — is the firm. Calibre is what happens when the firm's discipline is turned into a product. They share a spine and a typographic voice; they diverge only in colour, so a reader always knows which one is speaking.

Boutique finance & decision advisory. Small senior teams; every engagement leaves a working artifact. Palette: cream ground, rust accent. Chapter numerals set in §.

CalibreBY DAFTAR
THE PRODUCT · HIRING DIAGNOSTIC

The firm's rigour, productised — scorecards, a shared reading method, team calibration. Palette: white ground, deep-green accent. Always carries the by Daftar kicker.

THE RULE

Parent and product never share a ground colour on the same surface. Cream belongs to Daftar; white belongs to Calibre. When Calibre appears inside a Daftar page, it sits on a green band — never the reverse.

§ 05 — Voice

Say the thing others hedge.

Both brands write the same way: plainly, with the working shown. Confidence without decoration. No cadence claims until the record earns them.

01

Write it down.

If a decision can't be written in a form that holds up, it isn't finished.

02

Evidence over opinion.

Show the working rather than defend a verdict on authority.

03

One title, everywhere.

Ahmad is Founder across every surface — cover, letters, LinkedIn, capability statements. “Principal” is retired.

04

Leave a tool behind.

Every engagement ends with something the client can run themselves.

§ 07 — Collateral

The tri-fold, in both voices.

One brochure architecture serves both brands: a landscape tri-fold, two sides of three panels, dashed fold lines, printed at 11×8.5in. The parent leads its own sheet; the product leads the other. Each closes on a single dark panel that hands off to the sister brand — Daftar's inside closes on green Calibre; Calibre's inside closes on ink Daftar.

FRONT COVER · INKThe decisions you'll defend.

FRONT COVER · GREENHire on evidence, not a hunch.
CALIBRE BROCHURE

White ground, green accent. Outside: pilot CTA · why-Calibre · cover. Inside: the problem · four-step method · Daftar panel (ink).

THE RULE

A brochure never mixes grounds within a panel. The hand-off panel is the only place the sister brand appears, and it always sits on that sister's dark surface — green for Calibre, ink for Daftar — never the reverse.

§ 08 — Iconography

Drawn like the rules.

Icons are thin line marks on a 24px grid — the same 1.5px hairline as our dividers, drawn as if ruled. Single stroke, round caps and joins, no fills, no duotone, no corner flourish. They label; they never decorate.

§ 09 — Social templates

The same page, at 1080.

Feed posts are the handbook page shrunk to a square: a numbered mono kicker, a serif line with one accent word, and the wordmark locked bottom-left. Built on a 1080×1080 grid with a 72px margin. Daftar posts on cream; Calibre posts on white; the number series (№ 001…) threads a campaign together.

§ 10 — Motion

It settles; it never bounces.

Motion behaves like ink drying, not like a spring. Elements arrive along one restrained ease, fades and reveals are short, and nothing overshoots. When in doubt, move less — the record is calm.

DURATION SCALE
120ms · tap
200ms · hover
320ms · reveal
560ms · page
THE HOUSE EASE
Settle
cubic-bezier
(0.2, 0.8, 0.2, 1)

One curve for nearly everything: quick to leave, slow to land, no return past the target.

DO
  • ▸Fade + 8px rise for entrances; reverse to exit.
  • ▸Stagger lists by 40ms, never more.
  • ▸Respect prefers-reduced-motion.
DON'T
  • ▸No bounce, elastic, or spring overshoot.
  • ▸No spinning loaders — use a quiet bar.
  • ▸Nothing longer than 560ms in the UI.
